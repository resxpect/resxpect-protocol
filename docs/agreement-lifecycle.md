# Agreement Lifecycle

A RESXPECT agreement is designed to create a verifiable path from an initial agreement to an eventual outcome.

## Core Flow

A typical agreement follows this path:

**Creation → Funding → Start Work → Evidence → Review → Completion / Dispute**

Each stage plays a role in connecting work performed to an eventual outcome and, where applicable, reputation.

## 1. Creation

An agreement begins when the terms of work are defined between the parties.

This establishes the basis of the agreement, including what is expected, who is involved and what the intended outcome should be.

## 2. Funding

Before work proceeds, the agreement may require funding or confirmation that the value attached to the agreement has been committed.

This helps create clarity around the seriousness and structure of the arrangement.

## 3. Start Work

Once the agreement is active, the work phase begins.

This is the point at which the Runner can proceed with the agreed task or service.

## 4. Evidence

As work progresses or once work is completed, evidence may be submitted to support what has been done.

Evidence may include written submissions, files, images, source outputs or other relevant proof depending on the nature of the work.

## 5. Review

The submitted work and evidence are reviewed against the original agreement.

This stage allows the parties to determine whether the work has been completed as expected.

## 6. Completion

If both sides agree that the work has been delivered satisfactorily, the agreement is completed.

Successful completion may contribute to reputation according to the rules of the protocol.

## 7. Dispute

If the parties do not agree on the outcome, the agreement may enter dispute resolution.

In this case, the protocol can rely on neutral review processes to assess the agreement, the submitted evidence and the outcome being contested.

## Why the lifecycle matters

The lifecycle is important because RESXPECT is not designed around isolated ratings or unsupported claims.

It is designed to connect:

**Agreement → Work → Evidence → Outcome → Reputation**

This structure allows reputation to emerge from completed, reviewable agreements rather than from opinion alone.
