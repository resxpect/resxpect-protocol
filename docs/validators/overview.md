# Validators

Validators provide an independent review layer within the RESXPECT protocol.

Their role is to help protect agreement quality, reputation integrity and fair resolution when an agreement cannot be concluded normally.

## Core Validator Responsibilities

Validators operate across three main areas:

### Precheck Validation

Precheck Validation provides additional review before certain agreements proceed.

Its purpose is to identify agreements that may require closer assessment before work begins.

### Reputation Validation

Reputation Validation checks whether reputation being generated through completed agreements is supported by the work and evidence behind it.

This helps ensure that reputation remains connected to genuine activity rather than unsupported claims.

### Dispute Resolution

Dispute Resolution is used when the parties to an agreement cannot reach the same conclusion about the outcome.

Validators review the agreement and relevant evidence to provide a neutral resolution.

## Independent Review

Validators are separate from the Creator and Runner involved in the agreement being reviewed.

Their role is not to perform the work or control the agreement, but to assess information according to the rules of the protocol.

## Validator Incentives

Validators may receive stablecoin rewards for completing qualifying review work.

The reward structure varies depending on the type of validation being performed.

## Protocol Integrity

The Validator system is intended to provide additional assurance at points where an agreement, evidence or reputation requires independent review.

Together, the three Validator functions cover:

**Agreement Safety → Reputation Integrity → Resolution**

## Public Specification

This repository documents the public responsibilities and decision framework of Validators.

Security-sensitive selection mechanisms, anti-collusion systems, internal monitoring and production implementation remain private.
