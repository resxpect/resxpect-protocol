# Reputation Validation

Reputation Validation protects the integrity of reputation generated through RESXPECT.

Its purpose is to check that reputation is supported by genuine work and appropriate evidence rather than being created from unsupported or misleading activity.

## Purpose

Completing an agreement does not automatically mean that every reputation signal behind it should be accepted without review.

At defined points in the protocol, completed agreements may be subject to independent Validator review.

This creates a connection between:

**Agreement → Evidence → Validation → Reputation**

## What Validators Review

Reputation Validation focuses on three core checks.

### Skill Relevance

Does the completed agreement genuinely relate to the skill under which reputation is being generated?

### Evidence Validity

Does the submitted evidence reasonably support the work and outcome being claimed?

### Evidence Integrity

Is the evidence consistent with a genuine completed agreement and suitable for supporting reputation?

## Validator Review

Validators assess the available agreement and evidence independently.

Their role is not to decide whether they personally like the work.

Their role is to determine whether the reputation being generated is properly supported by the record behind it.

## Validation Outcome

Where the required validation standard is met, qualifying reputation can remain part of the participant's record.

Where validation fails, reputation associated with the affected activity may be withheld or removed according to the protocol rules.

## Why Reputation Validation Matters

Without independent validation, a reputation system can become vulnerable to reputation farming, fabricated activity or agreements that do not genuinely support the skill being represented.

Reputation Validation helps preserve the principle that:

**Reputation should represent work that can be supported by evidence.**

## Relationship to Other Validator Functions

**Precheck Validation**  
Protects agreements before work begins.

**Reputation Validation**  
Protects reputation after work has been completed.

**Dispute Resolution**  
Resolves disagreement about an agreement outcome.

## Public Specification

This document describes the public principles of Reputation Validation.

Internal abuse-detection methods, anti-farming systems, Validator selection security and other security-sensitive implementation details are not disclosed in this repository.
