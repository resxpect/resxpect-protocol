# Dispute Resolution

Dispute Resolution provides a neutral process for agreements that cannot be concluded normally.

It is used when the Creator and Runner disagree about whether the agreed work was completed correctly or what outcome should follow.

## Purpose

A disagreement should not depend entirely on one party having the final say.

Dispute Resolution allows independent Validators to review:

- The original agreement
- The agreed scope
- Submitted evidence
- The disputed outcome

The aim is to reach a resolution based on the agreement and the record behind it.

## Core Flow

**Disagreement → Dispute → Validator Review → Final Resolution**

## Validator Review

Validators assess the available information independently.

Their responsibility is to determine which outcome is best supported by the agreement and evidence rather than act on behalf of either participant.

## Possible Outcomes

A dispute may result in different settlement outcomes depending on what the Validator review establishes.

The protocol distinguishes between situations where:

- The Runner's position is supported
- The Creator's position is supported
- The evidence supports an outcome between those two positions

This allows the protocol to handle disputes that are not always completely one-sided.

## Evidence

Evidence plays a central role in dispute resolution.

Validators review what was originally agreed alongside the evidence provided for the work.

This creates a direct relationship between:

**Agreement → Evidence → Review → Resolution**

## Reputation

The outcome of a dispute may affect reputation where the protocol determines that one party's position was clearly supported or clearly unsupported.

Some outcomes may remain reputation-neutral where the evidence does not justify assigning clear responsibility to either side.

## Finality

Once a dispute reaches its final protocol outcome, the resolution becomes part of the agreement record.

This provides a defined end point rather than allowing the same disagreement to continue indefinitely.

## Relationship to Other Validator Functions

**Precheck Validation**  
Protects agreements before work begins.

**Reputation Validation**  
Protects the integrity of reputation generated through completed work.

**Dispute Resolution**  
Provides neutral resolution when an agreement outcome is contested.
