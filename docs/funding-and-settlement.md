# Funding and Settlement

Funding and settlement define how value moves through a RESXPECT agreement.

The aim is to ensure that work begins against committed funds and that the final financial outcome remains connected to the agreement and its evidence.

## Purpose

A Runner should not have to rely only on a promise that payment will be made after the work is completed.

RESXPECT is designed so that funds associated with a protected agreement are committed before the work proceeds.

This creates a clearer relationship between:

**Agreement → Funding → Work → Evidence → Outcome → Settlement**

## Funding

Before protected work begins, the value attached to the agreement is committed.

This confirms that the agreement has been financially backed before the Runner starts work.

Funding is therefore part of the agreement lifecycle rather than something that happens only after delivery.

## Protected Agreement

Once the agreement is funded and active, the committed funds remain connected to that agreement while the work is being performed.

Neither party should be able to unilaterally change the agreed financial outcome outside the rules of the protocol.

The agreement record, evidence and eventual outcome determine how settlement proceeds.

## Successful Completion

When the agreed work is delivered and accepted, the agreement can proceed to successful settlement.

The Runner receives the amount due according to the agreement and applicable protocol rules.

The completed financial outcome remains connected to the underlying agreement record.

## Rejected Delivery

If the Creator determines that the agreed outcome has not been met, the delivery may be rejected.

The Runner may accept that rejection, allowing the agreement to conclude according to the applicable settlement rules.

If the Runner does not agree with the rejection, the agreement may proceed to Dispute Resolution.

## Dispute Settlement

When the parties cannot agree on the final outcome, the protected funds remain subject to the dispute process.

Validators review the agreement and relevant evidence.

The final dispute outcome determines how the protected value is settled according to the protocol.

This allows the financial result to follow the same record used to assess the disagreement.

## Stablecoin Settlement

RESXPECT is designed around settlement using **USDC on Base**.

Using a stablecoin allows agreement values, payouts and Validator rewards to remain denominated in a comparatively stable unit rather than relying on a volatile protocol token.

## Protocol Fees

Certain successful agreement outcomes may include protocol fees.

Any applicable fee forms part of the settlement rules governing the agreement.

Detailed fee schedules may be documented separately as the public protocol specification develops.

## Agreement Record

Settlement is not intended to exist separately from the agreement.

The final financial outcome remains connected to:

- The original agreement
- The work performed
- The evidence submitted
- The review or dispute outcome
- The resulting reputation record where applicable

This preserves a verifiable relationship between what was agreed and how the agreement ultimately concluded.

## Public Specification

This document describes the public principles governing funding and settlement within RESXPECT.

It does not disclose production smart-contract implementation, private keys, administrative controls, wallet security systems, internal transaction handling or other security-sensitive infrastructure.
